# Solmar Bay — Make GTA 6 (gta6-01)

Solmar Bay is an original top-down open-world crime game: a coastal city of eight districts, driveable (and stealable) cars, traffic, pedestrians, a five-star wanted system, police chases on foot and in cruisers, and five story jobs from fixer Dario Vane. Everything is hand-written JavaScript on a canvas — no libraries, no network, no audio files.

## Play it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in your browser.

## How to play

- **WASD / arrows** — walk (Shift to run), drive when in a car
- **E** — enter / exit a car, start a job at Vane's Garage
- **Mouse + click** — aim and shoot (on foot)
- **R** — reload · **Space** — handbrake · **P / Esc** — pause · **M** — mute
- **ENTER** — start · **C** — continue your saved game

Steal a car and the driver runs — and someone calls it in. Rack up heat and the Solmar Bay police send cruisers after you, and foot cops if you are walking. Lose them, and the heat fades. Complete all five jobs for Dario Vane to own the city.

Solmar Bay is an original game. Not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Grand Theft Auto.
